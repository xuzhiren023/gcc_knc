M4 (S13-01, 7120 full): held-out bzip2 0.927x vs GCC 5.1.1. Diagnosis docs/research/m4-bzip2-diagnosis.md: in mainGtU's inner loop the register allocator keeps byte c1 in mask register k0 (16 kmov per iteration, AGI stalls before loads) because knc_processor_costs prices mask_to_integer/integer_to_mask at 1 (i386-knc.cc:158,160), below a GPR move (2) and a spill (4); -mtune=generic (6) removes every kmov. Fix in general: raise both to at least 4 (6 safe), red T1 first (a byte loop that must not use k registers for integer values), model-first (knc-twin predicts 1.5-2.0 s of the 5.38 s gap), T0-T2, ICE sweeps, x86 identity by the lead, and check no regression in mask-heavy vector code (masked tails, compares, gathers). Owner rule: never slower than GCC 5.1.1. Never tune on bzip2 itself (held out): use a development-corpus or synthetic byte loop for the test; bzip2 is confirmed only at the M5 measurement.
