Backlog E4-01, part 2. Design: ADR-0007. With S4-02's compares in place, add what the vectorizer
needs to if-convert and vectorize these on `zmm`, for V16SF, V8DF and V16SI (and V8DI where
ADR-0007 says the compare exists):
- `c[i] = a[i] > b[i] ? x[i] : y[i]`, and `if (a[i] > 0) c[i] = x[i];` (masked store or blend,
  as ADR-0007 chooses);
- `fabs` (sign-bit logic or a blend), and `fmin`/`fmax` plus the `a < b ? a : b` idiom, with
  `-O2 -ftree-vectorize` semantics (no `-ffast-math`): NaN and signed zero exactly as the scalar
  code gives them;
- conditional arithmetic (`if (m[i]) c[i] += x[i];`), if ADR-0007 maps `cond_<op>`.
