Backlog E4-04, part B (split from S4-05 on 2026-11-04). The lead's implementation plan is in
`docs/research/s4-05-plan.md`, part B. Read it first.

Today S4-03's KNC `maskload`/`maskstore` emit an aligned masked `vmov*{k}` for 64-byte aligned
addresses and an element-wise fallback otherwise, and a KNC `add_stmt_cost` term keeps misaligned
masked loops scalar. Replace the element-wise fallback with the guarded aligned split from ADR-0007
point 7:
- lines `L0 = A & -64` and `L1 = L0 + 64`, and shift `s = (A & 63) / elem`;
- the data rotated with one IMCI permute;
- masks `(k << s)` and `(k >> (L - s))` computed in a GPR;
- at most two aligned masked `vmov*{k}`, each skipped with `jkzd` when its mask is empty, and merged
  for loads.

Every line it names must then hold an active element (S3-03's fault rules). Then decide, with
evidence, whether the `add_stmt_cost` term can be relaxed so misaligned conditional loops vectorize.
If the split costs more than the scalar loop, keep the term.

Use `vpermd` for the rotation (`valignd` takes only an immediate shift, plan item 5). To force the misaligned path in tests, add `--param vect-max-peeling-for-alignment=0` (main loop) or `--param vect-partial-vector-usage=1` (epilogues) to `-fvect-cost-model=unlimited`.

The lead ran `s4_masks` at `-fvect-cost-model=unlimited` on the card after S4-04: S4-03's element-wise fallback is exact and stays inside the arrays (`claude-48d96b03…`). The split must keep that result.
