Backlog E1-06. Milestone M0 asks: can GCC 16 be our base, on this host? Go if `cc1` builds for
`k1om`, its assembly is accepted by the 2.22 assembler, and an incremental rebuild plus T1 takes
5 minutes or less (`docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md`, section 6). S0-06 measured 169 s to rebuild after
touching `i386.cc` and 265 s after a comment in `i386.md`, for a stock x86-64 build.

Deliverable: `docs/research/m0-evidence.md`, short, with numbers and log paths.

  timed end to end, three runs each
