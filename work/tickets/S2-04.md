Backlog E2-02. KNC has no direct move between general registers and `zmm`, so every scalar
transfer goes through memory, and the one-lane mask needs a scratch mask register and a general
register. Under register pressure this needs correct secondary reloads; the donor drops the
address setup on deleted stores (`tests/regress/knc-secondary-reload.f90`, compile-only; its
header gives the RTL symptom check).

Acceptance (T1 and T2):

- [ ] Red first: the reproducer wired in as a T1 test with its header's RTL check, and a C
  mixed-pressure test, failing or shown missing before the change
- [ ] T1: no direct GPR-to-`zmm` or `zmm`-to-GPR move in any output; in a function with all
  general registers live across FP transfers, every transfer still has its address and mask set
- [ ] The reproducer passes on GCC 16, whether or not the donor's mechanism exists there
  (`CURRENT_STATE.md` has the lead's reading of `lra-constraints.cc`)
- [ ] Native, once the card is free: the mixed-pressure test gives the same results as the host
  reference
