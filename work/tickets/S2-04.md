transfer goes through memory, and the one-lane mask needs a scratch mask register and a general
register. Under register pressure this needs correct secondary reloads; the donor drops the
address setup on deleted stores (`tests/regress/knc-secondary-reload.f90`, compile-only; its
header gives the RTL symptom check).
