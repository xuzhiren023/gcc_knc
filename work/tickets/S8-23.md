S8-11's paired M2 (lead card run 2026-11-10 23:39Z, Sprint 8 head fe7ed380f149 vs Sprint 7
497bbe8bedf0, `docs/research/card-runs-s8.md`): every kernel is faster or equal except **short**,
1.050-1.097 slower at L2 and memory, densities 1 and 4, both launches. S8-20 recovered about 2% of it
(short 0.977-0.986 against S8-15). short runs one vector iteration per call, so any fixed per-call or
per-loop cost added in Sprint 8 shows in full.

Deliver:
- Bisect the Sprint 8 defaults on the short kernel's assembly and on a host-side instruction count:
  S8-03 prefetch (prefetches issued in a one-iteration loop), S8-08 function alignment, S8-09 cost
  model and stride versioning, S8-13 streaming-store guard, S8-14 data alignment, S8-15/S8-20 unroll,
  S8-19, S8-21. Use each default's `-fno-*`/`--param` off switch on the knc head.
- Fix the cause in KNC tuning without losing the gains on the other kernels (for example no prefetch
  when the loop is known or estimated to run fewer iterations than the prefetch distance).
- Stage the M2 pair against the knc head at densities 1 and 4 for the lead.

Acceptance:

- [ ] Red first: a T1 scan of the short kernel's loop for the overhead found (for example no
  `vprefetch` in a loop the vectorizer knows runs once)
- [ ] The cause named with assembly in the commit
- [ ] Card (lead): short no slower than Sprint 7 beyond the A/A bias; the other kernels keep their gains
