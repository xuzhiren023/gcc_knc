Owner (2026-11-10): use the compiler to reorder instructions so the in-order KNC core hides latency
("as out-of-order as possible"), measured on the card; and check it does not fight the in-order
tuning already done. ADR-0009 point 4 and backlog E5-05/E7-04 plan this; S6-06 added a KNC pipeline
description (`gcc/config/i386/knc.md`), which then made M2 mat8 10% slower (`card-runs-s6.md`); it
has not been re-measured since Sprint 8.

Deliver (flags only, no compiler change):
1. Report what the current compiler does at `-O2 -march=knc`: is the first scheduling pass
   (`-fschedule-insns`, before register allocation) on or off; is the second (`-fschedule-insns2`)
   on; is `knc.md` used (dump `-fsched-verbose` on one kernel).
2. Builds of the M2 kernels and of two scalar kernels (a dependent integer chain with loads, and a
   scalar FP loop with x87 latency) in five variants:
   - `-O2 -march=knc` (baseline);
   - no scheduling (`-fno-schedule-insns -fno-schedule-insns2`), to see what scheduling buys today;
   - `-fschedule-insns` (pre-RA scheduling) and `-fschedule-insns -fsched-pressure` (pressure-aware);
   - `-fschedule-insns -fsched-pressure` with `--param knc-threads-per-core=1` (more unroll and chains).
   Report spills (count of stack loads and stores in the hot loops) per variant.
3. A paired timing runner (ADR-0010) at 1 and 4 threads per core, M2 kernels at L2, scalar kernels
   single-thread pinned, plus the scalar kernels at 4 copies on one core.

Acceptance:

- [ ] Item 1 answered with the dump evidence
- [ ] Binaries and runner staged, checksums equal across variants; no card job by the engineer
- [ ] A short prediction per kernel from the assembly (more overlap, more spills, or no change), so the
  lead can read the card numbers against it
