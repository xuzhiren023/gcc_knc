   - `-fschedule-insns -fsched-pressure` with `--param knc-threads-per-core=1` (more unroll and chains).

3. A paired timing runner (ADR-0010) at 1 and 4 threads per core, M2 kernels at L2, scalar kernels
   single-thread pinned, plus the scalar kernels at 4 copies on one core.
