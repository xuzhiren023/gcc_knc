Lead 2026-11-16. Owner rule (relayed by knc-boinc, 2026-11-16): any speed gain the compiler can make is made in
GCC-KNC, not by hand in the application. Tonight's dominant term in MilkyWay N-body on the card is one-thread scalar
double code: the serial tree build takes 13.2 s on the card against 1.1 s on one laptop core, and mixed-dwarf body
generation ~4,560 s serial on the laptop. ADR-0004 keeps scalar FP bodies on x87 (arguments and results in zmm lane 0,
transfers through memory). The proposal: compute scalar double (and float) with masked IMCI operations in lane 0 of a
zmm register, removing x87 stack traffic and zmm/x87 transfers. This ticket decides go or no-go; no GCC change.

Model-first, standard tier per op, deep tier per region (`.claude/skills/model-first/`, knc-optimize installed skill):
1. Per-operation cost table, x87 vs lane-0 IMCI, for add, sub, mul, FMA (IMCI only), compare+branch, convert to/from
   int, load/store, div and sqrt (IMCI: the S10-09 correctly rounded sequences), and the zmm<->x87 transfer the
   current ABI pays. Latency and reciprocal throughput at 1 and 2 threads per core, from
   `docs/research/knc-machine.yaml` and `knc-characterization.md`; where a value is missing, rank with knc-twin
   (installed skill, repo `C:/Users/xuzhi/Documents/knc-twin`) and mark it as a prediction.
2. Three regions: knc-boinc's tree build (`nbMakeTree`; ask the knc-boinc session for its reduced repro or the built
   object via the lead), one M4 x87-heavy loop (PB-ludcmp or NPB-EP from S12-06/S12-08), and one zlib/bzip2-style
   integer-plus-FP mix. For each: x87 schedule today (from our -O2 -march=knc assembly), a hand-written lane-0 IMCI
   version of the same work (host-assembled, knc_lint clean, not run on the card), bound and twin prediction for both.
3. Semantics: rounding (IMCI double rounds once to 53 bits; x87 PC=53 keeps the extended exponent range, so overflow,
   underflow and subnormal results can differ), flags and exceptions, -frounding-math, errno, long double (stays
   x87), and what changes for bit-identity against x86-64 SSE2 builds (likely better). Cite ADR-0003/0004 and the
   ISA manual by page; ADR-0013 rules for vendor text.
4. Verdict in `docs/research/s14-44-lane0-scalar.md`: go if the bound shows at least 1.5x on the tree build or at
   least 5% whole-program on two M4 programs, with no FP-contract loss; then list the GCC work (ABI unchanged or
   ADR-0004 amendment, md patterns, reload/spill classes, x87 kept for long double) and its forward-port cost. No-go
   otherwise, with the numbers.
