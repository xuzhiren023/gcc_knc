Backlog E5-07; ADR-0009 point 5 and amendment 1 point 2. KNC's L2 hardware prefetcher follows at
most 16 streams and stops at 4 KB pages, and the in-order core stalls on every miss, so software
prefetch carries most streaming code (WRF measured 2.4x on short rows; ICC uses 64/32 iterations).
Inputs: `docs/research/knc-characterization.md` section "Sprint 8 tuning checks" (S8-01): C2 (does `vprefetch` pair with a vector op), C4 (TLB reach), C5 (distance sweep on a
stream, a stencil and a short-row kernel at 2 and 4 threads per core).

Deliver:
- `vprefetch0`/`vprefetch1` (and `vprefetche*` for written streams if C1/C5 support it) as
  prefetch patterns in `imci.md`, used by GCC's loop prefetching;
- `-fprefetch-loop-arrays` on by default for `-march=knc` at `-O2`/`-O3` with KNC parameters:
  an L2 distance greater than the L1 distance, both from C5's best points and scaled by
  `knc-threads-per-core`; L2 prefetch also for unit-stride loops with more streams than
  16 / threads-per-core; a page-crossing prefetch for the first lines of the next 4 KB page if C5
  shows it pays;
- a documented `--param` for each distance.

Acceptance:

- [ ] Red first: T1 scans on a stream, a stencil and a short-row loop that require both prefetch
  levels at the chosen distances, and one that requires no prefetch for a loop that fits in L1
- [ ] The lead's paired card run shows the stream and stencil kernels at least as fast, and the
  short-row kernel faster (decide.py), at 2 and 4 threads per core
- [ ] The M2 kernels, `s4_masks` and `s5_ops` still pass on the card; the lead measures the M2
  kernels before and after with the S6-15 paired fixture (`build.py --before`, `decide.py`); no M2
  kernel slower beyond the 1.6% A/A bias, and claims under 3% get a link-order check (ADR-0010)
- [ ] T0/T1/T2 otherwise unchanged; torture changes listed; x86 assembly identical (`-O2` and
  `-O2 -mavx512f`); shared i386 lines and hunks reported (ADR-0006 forms only)
- [ ] No card job by the engineer; stage binaries under `.work/_shared/<ticket>/` for the lead
