ADR-0006, rule 1: shared i386 files hold hooks, not code. Several Sprint 2 and 3 changes put KNC
bodies in shared files, which makes them conflict on upstream updates. A trial merge onto
upstream `master` (2026-11-01) conflicted in `i386-protos.h` because of one of them.

Move, with no change in behaviour:

- `ix86_expand_knc_cas` from `i386-expand.cc` to `i386-knc.cc`, with its prototype in
  `i386-knc.h`
- the `switch` on modes at the top of `ix86_expand_move` (`gen_knc_move*`) into an
  `ix86_knc_expand_move` in `i386-knc.cc`, behind one `if (TARGET_KNC && ix86_knc_expand_move (op0,
  op1)) return;`
- every other KNC prototype in `i386-protos.h` to `i386-knc.h`
- any other shared-file block longer than about five lines that is KNC-only (list them first:
  `git diff releases/gcc-16.2.0 knc -- gcc/config/i386 gcc/common/config/i386`, excluding the KNC
  files)

Acceptance:

- [ ] Shared lines go down, measured the sprint's way, before and after. The number of hunks in
  shared files does not go up
- [ ] Generated code is unchanged: identical assembly over the torture compile files at `-O0` and
  `-O2`, and over `tests/t1` with `--vector`, before and after
- [ ] `tools/check t0 t1 t2 --compiler gcc16` gives the same results as before
- [ ] No new tests: the identical-assembly check is the test
