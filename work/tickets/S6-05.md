Keep the scattered-index gather test as red decision 1 (table point 5: 16 lines cost about 127 per core, so it must not be costed as one op). Do not add a new mechanism. Rework knc-cost-reduce.c for this instead of the fixed-8 case and drop -fno-tree-slp-vectorize.
