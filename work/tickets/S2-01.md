Backlog E2-01, design part. GCC 16's i386 backend ties the 512-bit modes, `xmm16`-`xmm31` and the
mask registers `k0`-`k7` to AVX-512F, and the scalar ABI to SSE. KNC has 32 `zmm` and 8 mask
registers but neither SSE nor AVX-512. S2-02 to S2-05 need one design for exposing them; the lead
turns this note into an ADR. No fork change in this ticket.

The donor's convention is in `docs/sprints/SPRINT-02.md`. The donor's changes are in
`.work/_shared/gcc-donor` and `docs/research/donor-inventory.md` (rows for `i386.c`, `i386.md`,
`knc.md`, all marked rewrite).

Deliverable: `docs/research/knc-register-design.md`, short, every claim with a `file:line` in the
GCC 16 fork or the donor.

Acceptance:

- [ ] Where GCC 16 gates the `zmm`, extended `xmm` and mask registers, the 512-bit modes, and the
  SF/DF argument and return classes on SSE or AVX-512F, and what each gate controls
- [ ] How the donor enabled them for KNC, and which of its choices still fit GCC 16
- [ ] A recommended design: which registers, modes and classes become available under
  `TARGET_KNC`, how SSE stays off for code generation (no SSE instruction can be chosen), and
  where new patterns live (`imci.md`, per the inventory). Estimated changed lines in shared i386
  files: M1 allows 300 and 45 are used
- [ ] Callee-saved status of `zmm` and `k` registers in the donor's convention, and whether the
  donor's `%eax` and `%k2` scratch use is safe across calls
- [ ] Risks: anything that would let SSE or AVX-512 instructions be generated, with how a T1 test
  catches it
