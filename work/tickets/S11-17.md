(`docs/research/s9-18-pgo.md`); native microcases showed 19-21% less time in two cases and no gain in two. Before
Sprint 12 puts PGO on the development corpus, show it works on one complete program end to end: pick zlib or SQLite
(both development programs, branchy), build `-fprofile-generate -fprofile-update=atomic`, train on the card with a
training input distinct from the frozen evaluation input, rebuild `-fprofile-use`, and record missing or mismatched
profiles. The lead runs the card jobs. Output: a short section in `docs/research/s9-18-pgo.md` with what worked, what
broke, and the Sprint 12 plan. Never train on held-out programs.
