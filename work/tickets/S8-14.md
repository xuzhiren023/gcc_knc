Backlog E5-13; ADR-0009 point 5 and amendment 1 point 3. Vector code is fastest on 64-byte
lines (aligned masked tails in S8-02, full-line streaming stores in S8-13), and S8-12's C4 rerun
shows the first-level DTLB holds 64 4-KiB pages per core: a load beyond that costs about 29 ticks more.

Deliver, under `-march=knc` only:
- 64-byte alignment by default for static, global and local arrays of 64 bytes or more (the x86
  `-malign-data=cacheline` mechanism or the KNC data/local alignment hooks), keeping smaller objects
  and the ABI-visible layout of structs unchanged;
- the cache parameters the optimizers read set from the card: `l1-cache-line-size` 64, L1 32 KB/8-way,
  and the L2 size used for blocking and prefetch decisions = the smaller of L2 (512 KB) and the TLB reach
  (256 KB with 4-KiB pages), with the reason in a comment;
- gfortran `ALLOCATE` of arrays of vectorizable types aligned to 64 bytes if this is a small,
  contained change (with libgfortran's private runtime from Sprint 7); otherwise write why and
  leave it to backlog E6-02.

Acceptance:

- [ ] Red first: T1 scans that require `.align 64` (or `.p2align 6`) for a 64-byte-or-larger static,
  global and local array, and the unchanged alignment of a small object and of a struct member
- [ ] `--help=params`-style output (or a T1 scan of the dumped params) showing the KNC cache values
- [ ] The M2 kernels, `s4_masks` and `s5_ops` still pass on the card; the lead measures the M2
  kernels before and after with the S6-15 paired fixture (`build.py --before`, `decide.py`); no M2
  kernel slower beyond the 1.6% A/A bias, and claims under 3% get a link-order check (ADR-0010)
- [ ] T0/T1/T2 otherwise unchanged; torture changes listed; x86 assembly identical (`-O2` and
  `-O2 -mavx512f`); shared i386 lines and hunks reported (ADR-0006 forms only)
- [ ] No card job by the engineer; stage binaries under `.work/_shared/<ticket>/` for the lead
