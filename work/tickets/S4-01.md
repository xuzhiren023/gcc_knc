Backlog E4-01 and E4-04, design input for S4-02 to S4-05. Read only. Do not build GCC; reading
the source in `.work/_shared/gcc` (`knc`) and the generated files in `.work/_shared/gcc-build/gcc`
is enough.

Facts already settled:
- ADR-0004 keeps `k0`-`k7` allocatable under `TARGET_KNC`, holding HImode only.
- ADR-0005 has the four packed modes (V16SF, V8DF, V16SI, V8DI). It leaves `ix86_get_mask_mode`
  (`i386.cc`, near 26126) unchanged and partial vectors off (`i386-options.cc`, near 3187).
- S3-03 (`docs/research/knc-unaligned-probe.md`): masks never suppress a fault. An unaligned
  load or store half touches its whole 64-byte line whatever the mask; at a 64-byte aligned
  address the high half touches nothing.
- Manual 327364 is at `.work/_shared/intel-327364-001-xeon-phi-isa.txt`.

Answer, each with file:line or manual page:

1. **Mask mode.** What `get_mask_mode` must return for each packed mode under KNC, and which GCC
   16 mechanisms follow from that: AVX-512 style scalar-integer masks, `vec_cmp<mode><mask>`,
   `vcond_mask`, `maskload`/`maskstore`, and `cond_<op>` for conditional arithmetic. Which ones
   the vectorizer needs for (a) `if (a[i] > b[i]) c[i] = x[i]` (b) `?:` (c) `fabs`, `min`, `max`
   (d) `if` around a store.
2. **IMCI forms.** Compares (`vcmpps`/`vcmppd` predicates, `vpcmpd`/`vpcmpud`), with their NaN
   behaviour against C semantics (ordered/unordered, signalling). Blends (`vblendmps`/`vpblendmd`
   and the pd/q forms). Masked moves. Mask logic (`kand`, `kandn`, `kor`, `kxor`, `knot`,
   `kortest`, `kmov`, `kmerge2l1l`/`kmerge2l1h`). Mask to and from a GPR, and mask spills. What
   KNC lacks compared with AVX-512F (64-bit lane compares? `kshift`?), and how V8DI compares work.
3. **Carry forms.** `vpaddsetcd`, `vpadcd`, `vpsubsetbd` and `vpsbbd`: the sequence for V8DI add
   and sub from 32-bit lanes, and its swizzles (input for S4-04).
4. **Reuse audit.** Which AVX-512F mask patterns and expanders in `sse.md`, `i386.md` and
   `i386-expand.cc` could serve KNC through an ADR-0006 hook (condition term or entry delegation),
   and which must be new `imci.md` patterns because the encoding or semantics differ. Never widen
   an SSE or AVX pattern (ADR-0006 rule 4).
5. **Masked tails.** From the probe: when a masked vector remainder is safe. For example, the
   remainder's 64-byte line holds at least one array element, and the access is 64-byte aligned
   after the vectorizer's peel. Say what GCC 16 offers to express that (partial vectors with
   length or mask, epilogue choice, alignment peeling) and what must stay off. Lengths 0-65 at
   every element offset must stay correct, with arrays flush against a guard page.
6. **Line estimate** for S4-02 to S4-05 in shared i386 files, in ADR-0006 forms, and the hunks
   you expect.

Deliverable: `docs/research/knc-masks.md`, short, with one section per question and an "ADR-0007
inputs" list at the end. The lead writes and accepts ADR-0007 from it. Separate what you read from
what you infer.
