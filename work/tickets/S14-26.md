Lead 2026-11-16. S14-02 (deep, gcc lane) would add a KNC doloop pattern so SMS can run. Before anyone builds it,
bound the gain with knc-twin 0.4.0 (`knc-twin/references/handoff-prompt.md` first, then `knc-twin/SKILL.md`). Follow
`.claude/skills/model-first/SKILL.md`, tier standard. No GCC change, no GCC build, no card.

1. Take the innermost hot loops of at least five M4 programs from today's `-O2 -march=knc` builds compiled with the
   installed GCC-KNC (PB-gemm, PB-jacobi-2d, PB-heat-3d, NPB-MG, NPB-CG or HPCG SpMV, plus one scalar loop from zlib
   or SQLite). Use the program sources under `.work/S13-01/tools/corpus` or the corpus scripts. Do not read anything
   under `.work/_shared/m4-sealed/`.
2. Per loop: `from_binary.py --loop`, then `simulate` for today's schedule and `schedule` in modulo mode for the best
   overlapped schedule the twin finds. Record ResMII, RecMII, today's cycles per iteration, the modulo II, the
   register pressure it needs, result_class and status as printed.
3. Turn the per-loop gains into a whole-program bound with `rollup` and each loop's measured time share (from the M4
   profiles, or from a gprof or perf run on the host build if none exists; say which).

Decision rule (PLAN-M5-M6 principle 2): S14-02 is worth building only if the bound is at least 5% whole-program on at
least two programs. The answer is a table plus one paragraph in `docs/research/s14-26-sms-bound.md`: go, or no-go
with the reason (for example: loops already at RecMII, II gain eaten by spills). Unsupported or missing_parameter
statuses are results, not things to work around. Submit with the one-sentence note the owner reads.
