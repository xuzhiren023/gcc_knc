Owner (2026-11-11): complete the vector math to the set GCC vectorizes on x86 with glibc's libmvec
(27 functions in float and double: acos acosh asin asinh atan atan2 atanh cbrt cos cosh erf erfc exp exp10
exp2 expm1 hypot log log10 log1p log2 pow sin sincos sinh tan tanh). v0 (S10-02/S10-03) has exp, log, pow.

This ticket adds the functions whose algorithms are already copied in `contrib/knc-vecmath/native/translation/math_algorithms`
(ADR-0011): `cos_sin_e` (sin, cos), `tan_e` (tan), `log10_e` (log10), `atan_e` (atan, atan2), `hyperbolic_e`
(sinh, cosh, tanh and the inverses it implements), and whatever `bounded_f32`/`bounded_f64` implement (list them).
For each, in float (16 lanes) and double (8 lanes) where the copy provides it:
- the `__knc_vmath_<name>_<lanes>` entry point and its masked `_m` form in `libknc_vmath` (ADR-0011, ADR-0014);
- the vectorizer hook entry in `ix86_knc_veclibabi` (S10-03);
- a qualification job for `tests/vmath/card_qualify.py` with special cases from the C99 Annex F table, not the
  card's libm (S10-06), and the PhiWeather receipt as the target (ADR-0011 point 4; about 1 ULP).
