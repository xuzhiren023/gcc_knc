Questions:
1. **Build:** what `--enable-libgomp` needs for `k1om-mpss-linux` (`libgomp/configure.tgt`, the
   `config/linux/x86` futex and `cpu_relax`), and every KNC blocker you can find statically
   (SSE in `config/linux/x86`, `pause`, `cmpxchg16b`, `__builtin_ia32_*`, newer syscalls).
2. **Coexistence:** the card's Intel and GCC 5 OpenMP runtimes (`libiomp5`, `libgomp.so.1`):
   SONAMEs and symbol versions; how to guarantee a single OpenMP runtime per process, using the
   S7-06/S7-09 private-directory pattern.
3. **Affinity:** default places spread over physical cores and skip the OS core (logical CPU 0 and
   the last core); `OMP_PLACES`/`GOMP_CPU_AFFINITY` on KNC's numbering (core c = CPUs 4c+1..4c+4).
4. **Barrier and spin:** GCC 16's barrier (`config/linux/bar.c`); design a hierarchical barrier
   (siblings of a core first, then a tree across cores) and the `delay`-based spin with a spin
   count; where it plugs in; what to measure (check C11 from S8-10, barrier and fork at 60x4).
5. **Per-thread data:** padding of `gomp_thread`, team and work-share structures to 64 bytes;
   reductions privatized and combined in a tree; static schedule default.
6. **malloc:** per-thread arenas at 60-240 threads (address space under card_run's limits,
   contention); what libgomp should set (for example `M_ARENA_MAX`) or document.
