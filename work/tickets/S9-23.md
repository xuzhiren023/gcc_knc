Owner (2026-11-10, twice): card turns are exclusive, so our jobs have no memory limit. After S9-14,
`card_run` still applies an `RLIMIT_AS` to every job (`claude-pin`), sized from free memory under
`--profile full`. An address-space limit is not a memory limit: 240 threads reserve 64 MiB per glibc
arena and their stacks, so S9-20's M2 runner at 4 threads per core failed "out of memory" even under a
12,983 MiB limit (card 2026-11-11 05:00Z, `.work/_shared/tmp/claude-next8-out/s920b.out`), and earlier
240-thread results were limited the same way.

Deliver:
- In our exclusive turns (no other project's job running), apply no `RLIMIT_AS` unless a profile asks
  for one: `--profile 3100` and `5100` keep their limits (they stand in for smaller cards); `full`, no
  profile and the default get none. Keep the free-memory admission check and the 1.5 GiB margin, so a job
  does not start when the card is short of memory, and keep the shared-mode limits when another
  project's job overlaps.
- The receipt and ledger record "memoryLimitMiB": null for an unlimited job.
- `claude-pin` accepts "no limit" (for example 0) and skips `setrlimit`.

Acceptance:

- [ ] Red first: planner tests (no card): exclusive and no profile gives no limit; 3100/5100 give
  theirs; shared mode keeps the 10 GiB cap; `claude-pin` test for the unlimited case
- [ ] A hello job and a 240-thread malloc job staged for the lead; no card job by the engineer
