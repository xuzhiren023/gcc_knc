Facts already settled (do not re-derive):
- ADR-0005: the packed modes are V16SF, V8DF, V16SI and V8DI only. KNC never sets `TARGET_AVX512F`
  or any SSE or AVX flag, and there are no 128-bit or 256-bit vector modes on KNC.
- `imci.md` is read before `sse.md`, so it may not use names that `sse.md` defines. Shared i386
  changes take ADR-0006 forms: hooks, mode-tested condition terms, entry dispatch, and
  expander-only KNC copies of iterators.
- ADR-0003: vector results keep the scalar source semantics (no implicit fast-math).
- S3-03: masks never suppress faults, and unaligned pairs need element alignment.

Answer:

1. **IMCI conversion forms.**
   - The forms: `vcvtps2pd`, `vcvtpd2ps`, `vcvtdq2pd`, `vcvtudq2pd`, `vcvtfxpntdq2ps`,
     `vcvtfxpntudq2ps`, `vcvtfxpntps2dq`, `vcvtfxpntps2udq`, `vcvtfxpntpd2dq`, `vcvtfxpntpd2udq`,
     and any others.
   - For each: which lanes it reads and writes (the lower 8 of 16?), the rounding immediate and
     the exponent-adjust field, saturation or out-of-range results, NaN results, the exceptions
     raised, and its MXCSR use.
   - Map each form to C semantics: `(int)f` truncates toward zero, and `(float)i` rounds to
     nearest under the current mode. Say what a C program can observe.
   - List what has no IMCI form and stays scalar, for example 64-bit integer to and from double.
2. **GCC 16 entry points.**
   - The optabs: `fix_trunc<mode><vecint>2`, `float<vecint><mode>2`, `floatuns...`,
     `vec_unpacks_lo/hi`, `vec_unpacks_float_lo/hi`, `vec_unpack_sfix_trunc_lo/hi`,
     `vec_pack_trunc`, `vec_pack_sfix_trunc`, the integer `vec_unpacks/u_lo/hi`, and the vector
     forms of `extend<m><n>2` and `trunc<m><n>2`.
   - Which of them the vectorizer needs, and the vectorization factor each implies, for:
     - (a) `f[i] = i32[i]`;
     - (b) `i32[i] = f[i]`;
     - (c) `d[i] = f[i]` and `f[i] = d[i]`;
     - (d) mixed `float` and `double` arithmetic in one loop.
   - Which of these names `sse.md` already defines for V16SF, V8DF and V16SI (AVX-512F), and so
     need ADR-0006 handling.
3. **Up and down conversion in memory forms.**
   - Loads: `vmovaps` and `vmovdqa32` with `{uint8}`, `{sint8}`, `{uint16}`, `{sint16}` and
     `{float16}` up-conversion.
   - Stores: `vpackstore` and `vmovaps` with down-conversion.
   - Broadcast forms: `{1to16}` and `{4to16}`.
   - For each: alignment and fault footprint. For example, does an up-converting load of
     16 bytes read only 16 bytes, and what alignment does it need?
   - Whether down-conversion stores saturate or truncate.
4. **Byte and word data in GCC.**
   - KNC has no V64QI or V32HI modes. Say how the vectorizer could handle:
     - `int32 = uint8[i]` (zero-extend);
     - `uint8[i] = int32 & 255`;
     - `short` arrays;
     - `char` comparisons.
   - The candidates: extending loads (`zero_extend:V16SI (mem:V16QI)` with a memory-only V16QI
     mode?), `vec_unpacku`, or not at all.
   - What adding memory-only V16QI or V16HI modes would cost against ADR-0005: register classes,
     `ix86_vector_mode_supported_p`, and the x86 hooks that would then fire.
   - Recommend the smallest design that lets `char` and `short` loops vectorize, or say why they
     should stay scalar this sprint.
5. **Probe.**
   - Cover every correctness fact the manual leaves open:
     - conversion of NaN, infinity and out-of-range values;
     - the rounding mode the `fxpnt` forms use when their immediate says "current";
     - the flags raised;
     - the up-conversion fault footprint at a page end.
   - Write one C probe, `tests/regress/knc-convert-probe.c`, in the style of
     `tests/regress/knc-unaligned-probe.c` (guard pages, `sigsetjmp`).
   - Build it with the KNC GCC 16 at `-O0`, using inline asm, and give the build command in its
     header. The lead runs it on the card.
6. **Recommendation and estimate.** For each of E4-05 and E4-07, give:
   - the patterns needed in `imci.md`;
   - the shared hunks needed, with estimated lines and the ADR-0006 form for each;
   - the risks;
   - a proposed split into gcc tickets, with the T1 scans and native checks that would prove each.
