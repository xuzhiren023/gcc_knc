Backlog E5-01 groundwork and ADR-0009 point 2. Read only for GCC; you may write a probe under
`tests/regress/` and `docs/research/knc-measurement.md`. Do not build GCC. The lead runs every card
job; timing probes run as `tools/card_run --benchmark` jobs (exclusive card).

Answer, with Intel 330991/327364 pages, Klemm slide numbers from `docs/research/knc-microarch.md`,
or probe output:
1. Which clock to use for cycle counts and wall time on the card: `rdtsc` (is the TSC invariant
   and at what rate on this card; read `/proc/cpuinfo` and 330991), `clock_gettime`, and their
   cost and resolution.
2. Pinning: how `claude-pin` and `sched_setaffinity` map logical CPUs to cores and threads on this
   card (the S5-19 `cpuinfo` mapping), and how to place 1, 2, 3 and 4 threads per core over N cores.
3. Working sets: sizes that sit in L1 (32 KB per core, shared by its threads), L2 (512 KB per core)
   and memory, per thread density, with 64-byte alignment.
4. Repetitions: warm-up, the number of timed repetitions, and the statistic (median, minimum,
   spread) that gives a run-to-run variation under 2% for a streaming FP32 kernel. Measure it.
5. The M2 terms: "production thread density" (propose 4 threads per core on cores 0-59, and how
   many cores), "tested length" (propose the length set), and how a gain is computed against the
   x87 baseline.
6. What disturbs a timed run on this card (the OS core 60, the PhiWeather session's jobs, host
   transfers), and how `--benchmark` windows avoid it.

Deliver `docs/research/knc-measurement.md` with a proposed protocol the lead turns into ADR-0010,
and one probe program, `tests/regress/knc-timing-probe.c`, that measures items 1, 2 and 4. It
prints numbers only; the lead runs it.
