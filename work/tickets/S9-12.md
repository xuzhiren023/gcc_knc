ADR-0012 (owner, 2026-11-10): one fixed `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX` is wrong for some KNC cards (3120 6 GB,
5110P 8 GB, 7120 16 GB). libgomp on k1om sets glibc's arena cap at load from the memory the card
and the process can reserve, unless the user set `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX`.

Deliver:
- In libgomp's KNC config, at initialisation and before any worker starts: if `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX` is
  not in the environment, compute the cap per ADR-0012 point 3 and call `mallopt (M_ARENA_MAX, n)`.
  Inputs: `MemTotal` and `CommitLimit` (`/proc/meminfo`), `/proc/sys/vm/overcommit_memory`, the
  `RLIMIT_AS` soft limit, the CPUs of the affinity mask, the stack size threads will get
  (`OMP_STACKSIZE`/`GOMP_STACKSIZE`, else `RLIMIT_STACK`). Make the share of `MemTotal` and the
  minimum cap named constants: the lead sets them from measurement 7.
- The other card-dependent constants read the card too (owner 2026-11-10: defaults must suit 3120, 5110P
  and 7120): `gomp_knc_ncores`'s fallback of 60 (S9-09 barrier groups when no place list is built)
  comes from the online CPUs and the topology readers of `knc/affinity.c` (a 3120 has 57 cores);
  the default spin count's 1.1 GHz (`knc/spincount.h`, 3 ms of DELAY periods) comes from
  `cpu MHz` in `/proc/cpuinfo` (1238 on the 7120), with 1100 when it cannot be read.
- `omp_display_env` (verbose): the cap and its source (user, or derived with the budget).
- Measurement 7 staged for the lead: 240 threads (and 228, a 3120's 57 cores x 4) each doing
  100,000 `malloc`/`free` of 1 KiB and a mixed-size pattern, with the arena cap unset, 240, 120, 64,
  60, 30 and 8, and the derived default; at job limits of 6, 8 and 10 GiB (`card_run` config
  `memoryMB`). Report wall time, `VmPeak`, `VmRSS` and any allocation failure. ADR-0010 rules.

- glibc 2.21 has `M_ARENA_MAX` (`sysroot/usr/include/malloc.h:117-118`); it reads
  `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX` itself at its first malloc, so check the environment, not the current setting.
- The host test compiles the cap function on Cygwin x86_64 with a stub for the inputs.
- Card facts (2026-11-10): `card_run` applies its limit as `RLIMIT_AS`; the S9-06 `tasks` program
  failed at 240 threads under 6 GiB with the default arenas.
