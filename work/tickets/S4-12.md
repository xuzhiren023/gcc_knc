The owner watched the card on 2026-11-04: our cores 40-59 sat near idle during a suite run at
`-j 60`. `tools/card_run` `stage()` (around line 533) opens one SSH connection to upload each
`--file` (`send_file`, `cat > name`) and a second one to `chmod` it. A 300-program chunk costs
about 600 SSH round trips before anything runs; the programs then finish within seconds. The
Windows command line (32K) also caps a chunk at about 300 `--file` arguments
(`harness/card/run_suite.py`).

Fix:
1. **One upload per job.** Add `--file-list LIST` (one repo-relative path per line) beside
   `--file`. Pack every staged file into one stream in a simple format of your choice (name,
   mode, length, bytes), send it over one SSH connection, and unpack it on the card with a small
   static helper built like `claude-pin` (`tools/claude-pin.c`, built with the donor). BusyBox
   1.19.4 on the card lacks many tools (`docs/card-run.md`), so do not rely on `tar` unless a
   card probe by the lead shows it works. The helper writes only inside the job's run directory,
   rejects `..`, absolute and duplicate names (the same rules as `staging_name`), sets mode 700
   for ELF files and 600 for others, and fails closed on a short or malformed stream.
2. **Bigger chunks.** `harness/card/run_suite.py` passes `--file-list` and runs the whole suite
   (1,681 programs, about 24 MiB, within `maxStageMiB`) as one job at `-j 80 --limit-mb 80`, or in
   the fewest chunks the stage limit allows.
3. **Memory budget 10 GiB (owner, 2026-11-04: the other project rarely uses more than 7 GB).** One
   constant replaces the two hard-coded 7 GB limits in `card_run`; the >= 1.5 GB free-memory floor
   at admission stays. `run_suite.py` runs `-j 80 --limit-mb 96` (7,680 MiB).
4. Keep every other existing rule: ledger lines, cores 40-59, the memory admission, `/tmp/claude`,
   `claude-` names, cleanup on failure and on reboot.
