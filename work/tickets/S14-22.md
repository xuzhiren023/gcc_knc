Lead 2026-11-16. Window 10 lost its turn mid-window. Ledger rows 8214-8221: our turn row
claude-turn-s14-w10-20261116T042821 was queued at 04:28:21Z. The window script re-appended it every 2 h to stay
under card_run's 3-hour liveness (`active_jobs` / age check near tools/card_run:306-317), and the 06:28:27Z refresh
came after PhiWeather's queue row of 06:26:52Z. `own_turn` (tools/card_run:489) compares the latest queuedTime, so
our window now looked younger than their queue, and job w10-2 waited out `--timeout 300` with "their turn wait
expired; job was not staged" (exit 125).

Fix: a refreshed turn row keeps its original position. Accept an optional `firstQueuedTime` on claude-turn- rows:
order by it when present (fall back to queuedTime), and keep liveness on queuedTime. Card scripts that refresh a turn
row copy firstQueuedTime from the first row. Document both fields in the turn-row header comment of tools/card_run.
Rows without the field behave exactly as today, because PhiWeather's fifo_gate reads the same ledger.

Red first in the existing card_run tests: a ledger where our turn row is queued, a foreign queue arrives, and our
turn row is refreshed with firstQueuedTime from before the foreign queue gives own_turn true; the same ledger without
firstQueuedTime gives own_turn false (today's behaviour). No card job. Submit with the one-sentence note the owner
reads.
