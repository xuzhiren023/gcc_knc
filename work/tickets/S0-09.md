`loops()` in `tools/check.py` only recognises a branch with one operand. A KNC loop closed by
`jknzd %k1, .L2` or `jkzd %k2, .L2` has two, so it is not seen as a loop. The lead ran this on
2026-11-01: `jne .L2` finds 1 loop, and both mask branches find 0. A correct GCC 16 vector loop
would therefore fail T1 with "no packed `zmm` arithmetic". The Claude review of S0-03 reported
this first.

- [ ] `loops()` finds loops closed by `jknzd` and `jkzd`, and still finds ordinary conditional branches
- [ ] A test in `tests/regress/test_check.py` fails before the fix and passes after it, including a seeded passing case whose main loop closes with `jknzd`
- [ ] `tools/check t0 t1 t2` on the donor gives the same result as `tests/baseline/donor-gcc5.txt`
