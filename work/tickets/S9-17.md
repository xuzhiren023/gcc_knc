Best-practice review items 4 and 10 (`docs/research/knc-best-practice-review.md`). Add a short "Writing
fast code for KNC" section to `docs/gcc-knc-flags.md`, in our own words with source references (ADR-0013;
no Intel text):

- Threads per core: 4 for compute-bound loops; streaming code may do better at 1-2 per core (McCalpin
  STREAM, our C5 data); `--param knc-threads-per-core` to match.
- First-touch initialization by the thread that uses the data; fewer, larger parallel regions; `nowait`
  where safe.
- Heap: reuse large blocks instead of allocating per step; glibc's `MALLOC_MMAP_THRESHOLD_` and
  `MALLOC_TRIM_THRESHOLD_` (ICC had `-qopt-malloc-options`); the arena and stack notes from S9-12.
- Differences from ICC `-mmic` defaults: ICC flushed denormals (`-ftz`) and used faster divide and
  transcendental accuracy by default; GCC-KNC keeps IEEE (ADR-0003); how to opt in.
- Names: Intel® Xeon Phi™ with the trademark line (ADR-0013 point 9).
