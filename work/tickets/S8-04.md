mat8 a further 10% slower (`docs/research/card-runs-s6.md`, `docs/research/m2-result.md`). Use the
per-loop reports (S6-04) and assembly to find the cause first; write it in the ticket before code.

plus SLP is possible (Tian et al. Fig. 6.4), in the KNC cost hooks. If the cause is something else
(scheduling, spills, induction variables), fix that instead and say why.
