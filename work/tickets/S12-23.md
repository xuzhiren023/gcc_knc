- N06: `tests/t2/s7-07/fp-probe.c:81` multiplies 2^-126 by 2^-126; 2^-252 rounds to zero under gradual underflow too,
  so it cannot distinguish flushing. libgfortran/config/fpu-knc.h:263 generalizes it. Write probes with
  subnormal inputs and tiny results that differ between gradual underflow and FTZ/DAZ, for x87 and IMCI separately,
  and correct the claim from what they show (lead runs them on the card).
- N07: knc-crtfastmath.c:25 and the host state fixture misname MXCSR bits (R27 Table 2.15 p.39: 7-12 and 20 reserved,
  21 DUE; setting bit 20 is undefined). Fix the names and never set bit 20 in any fixture or runtime path.
- B16/B17: Fortran and libgcc clear and raise x87 status with FNSTENV/FLDENV or FNCLEX; erratum CD28 (R12 p.25)
  requires FNSAVE/edit/FRSTOR for clearing unmasked pending exceptions. Determine whether our paths can hit CD28
  (unmasked exceptions) and, if so, use the erratum's sequence; CD19 (faulting vector instruction may change MXCSR)
  gets a documented note.
