Backlog E4-07. Design: ADR-0008 point 7, which amends ADR-0005 point 1. Research:
`docs/research/knc-conversions.md` sections 3, 4 and 6 (ticket S5-gcc-C). Card facts
(`docs/research/card-runs-s5.md`): converted 16- and 32-byte accesses are safe at a page end, and
stores saturate, so keep the AND.

Deliver:
- V16QI and V16HI as supported transport-only modes, through a separate predicate (never
  `ix86_knc_packed_mode_p`), with the low 128 bits of `zmm` as their home, like TI.
- A KNC `TARGET_VECTORIZE_RELATED_MODE`.
- `ix86_knc_preferred_simd_mode` for QI and HI.
- The ABI hook rejecting the new modes.
- `*knc_<any_extend>v16qiv16si2`/`v16hiv16si2` from memory (`vmovdqa32 {u,s}int8/16`).
- `(float:V16SF (any_extend:V16SI (mem)))` as `vmovaps {...}`.
- `*knc_truncv16siv16qi2_store`/`v16hi` as `vpandd` plus a down-converting store.
- No byte arithmetic, compares or `movmisalign`.

- 2026-11-06 01:14 engineer ask: Blocked by ADR-0008 point 7: both modes must use low 128 zmm bits. i386-modes.def:85-86 defines V16QI as 16 bytes but V16HI as 32 bytes. Counterexample: two V16HI values identical in lanes 0-7 but different in lane 8 cannot survive low-128-bit transport/spill like TI. May V16HI use low 256 bits and 32-byte transfers, with V16QI using low 128 bits and 16-byte transfers? Please amend the ADR/ticket or specify a lossless representation. No edits or builds/tests; approved red f0f5332 remains.

- 2026-11-06 01:15 lead answer: Agreed; ADR-0008 point 7 is amended (commit on main): V16QI home is the low 128 bits with 16-byte transfers, V16HI the low 256 bits with 32-byte transfers. Register moves and spills copy the whole zmm. Memory transfers move exactly the value's size at natural alignment and stay inside one 64-byte line: vbroadcasti32x4 / vbroadcasti64x4 for loads, vpackstorelq with a 2- or 4-qword mask for stores. Never the unaligned unpack/pack pair; no movmisalign. Check the exact forms against Intel 327364-001 before use and cite the pages.
