Backlog E4-07. Design: ADR-0008 point 7, which amends ADR-0005 point 1. Research:
`docs/research/knc-conversions.md` sections 3, 4 and 6 (ticket S5-gcc-C). Card facts
(`docs/research/card-runs-s5.md`): converted 16- and 32-byte accesses are safe at a page end, and
stores saturate, so keep the AND.

Deliver:
- V16QI and V16HI as supported transport-only modes, through a separate predicate (never
  `ix86_knc_packed_mode_p`), with the low 128 bits of `zmm` as their home, like TI.
- A KNC `TARGET_VECTORIZE_RELATED_MODE`.
- `ix86_knc_preferred_simd_mode` for QI and HI.
- The ABI hook rejecting the new modes.
- `*knc_<any_extend>v16qiv16si2`/`v16hiv16si2` from memory (`vmovdqa32 {u,s}int8/16`).
- `(float:V16SF (any_extend:V16SI (mem)))` as `vmovaps {...}`.
- `*knc_truncv16siv16qi2_store`/`v16hi` as `vpandd` plus a down-converting store.
- No byte arithmetic, compares or `movmisalign`.
