S8-15 unrolls a vector loop of at most 16 insns up to 4 times at `-O2 -march=knc`. Its justification,
S8-01 C9, was measured at 1 and 2 threads per core. The lead's card run of 2026-11-10 (frozen set
`.work/_shared/tmp/claude-s8-15`, outputs `.work/_shared/tmp/claude-next2-out/s15-*.out`, M2 at L2,
paired against the S8-14/S8-09 knc objects, launch 1 / 2):

| Kernel | 1 thread/core | 4 threads/core |
|---|---|---|
| mat16 | 0.787 / 0.788 | 0.776 / 0.773 |
| stencil1 | 0.906 / 0.916 | 0.939 / 0.938 |
| affine_f32 | 0.961 / 0.959 | 1.005 / 1.017 |
| affine_f64 | 0.936 / 0.980 | **1.032 / 1.072** |
| stencil2 | 0.999 / 0.971 | 1.026 / 1.007 |
| short | 1.022 / 1.021 | 1.023 / 1.023 |
| mat8 | 0.998 / 1.000 | 1.006 / 1.002 |

At the default density (4 threads per core, `knc-threads-per-core=4`) the unroll costs affine_f64 more
than the 1.6% A/A bias and the 3% claim line, and short loses about 2% everywhere. S8-15's acceptance
("no M2 kernel slower beyond the 1.6% A/A bias") is not met at 4 threads per core.

Deliver:
- Make the unroll follow `knc-threads-per-core`: keep it where it gains (1-2 threads per core) and at 4
  threads per core keep only what does not lose, for example unroll 2, or no unroll for loops whose
  body is mostly memory traffic. Find the cause in the assembly of affine_f64 and short first
  (register pressure, prefetch distance per copy, remainder cost) and state it in the commit.
- Stage the M2 pair against the S8-16 knc head at densities 1 and 4, plus an A/A pair, for the lead.

Acceptance:

- [ ] Red first: T1 scans of the unroll factor at `--param knc-threads-per-core=1` and at the default 4
- [ ] The cause of the affine_f64 and short loss named with the assembly
- [ ] Stage the binaries for the lead's card run; no card job by the engineer. The lead's run must
  show no M2 kernel slower beyond the A/A bias at either density.
- [ ] T0/T1/T2 otherwise unchanged; x86 identical (the lead runs it)
