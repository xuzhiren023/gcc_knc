Backlog E3-02 and the aligned part of E3-03. Follow `docs/adr/0005-knc-packed-vector-modes.md`
(accepted) and `docs/research/knc-packed-modes.md`. Make V16SF, V8DF, V16SI and V8DI legal in
`zmm` under `TARGET_KNC`, with their moves. `preferred_simd_mode` stays `word_mode`, so nothing
auto-vectorizes yet; tests use GCC vector types (`__attribute__ ((vector_size (64)))`) through
pointers and locals. Tests go in `tests/t1/knc-vec-*.c` under S3-02's `--vector` selection.

First commit: the KNC-only `gcc/config/i386/i386-knc.cc` and `i386-knc.h` with their build
plumbing (ADR-0005 point 8). Changing `config.gcc` means one full rebuild, about 23 minutes at
`make -j8` with `-O2 -g1`; poll quietly. Instruction patterns go in `imci.md`; `sse.md` changes
only at entry points (ADR-0005 point 2).

**Shared i386 lines: allocation 24**, in the measure in `docs/sprints/SPRINT-03.md`. Report the
count at submit. If the change would pass 24, stop and ask.

Acceptance (T1, T2):

- [ ] Red first: for each mode, T1 tests that fail before the change: an aligned copy through
  pointers, a local live across a call, a zero and a non-zero constant, and a vector argument
- [ ] Loads, stores and register copies use only ADR-0005's aligned forms. Zero is `vpxord`. Other
  constants load from a 64-byte-aligned pool entry. Every output passes the S2 allowlist
  extended with those forms, has no `xmm`, `ymm`, `vmovups` or `{z}`, assembles with
  `--march=k1om` and lints clean, at `-O0` and `-O2`
- [ ] With all 32 `zmm` live across a call, every packed spill slot is 64-byte aligned from a
  16-byte-aligned caller, and every spill has its reload
- [ ] Vector-typed arguments, results and struct members of all four modes give "unsupported KNC
  register ABI mode", as XI does today
- [ ] No regression: `tools/check t0 t1 t2 --compiler gcc16` gives the same results as `knc` before
  the change, and the torture compile sweep at `-O2` shows no new ICE, assembler rejection or lint
  failure against `tests/baseline/gcc16-torture-compile.txt`
- [ ] Native, in S3-10: the copy, spill and constant tests print their expected values
