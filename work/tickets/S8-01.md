- **C1** write-only memory-sized output: `vmovaps` vs `vmovnraps` vs `vmovnrngoaps` plus a
  `lock addl` fence, at 1 core and 60 cores x 4 threads;
- **C2** does `vprefetch0`/`vprefetch1` pair with a vector op (U/V), or take an issue slot;
- **C4** page-stride loads at 128, 256, 384 and 448 KB per core, 1 and 4 threads (TLB reach);
- **C5** software prefetch distance sweep (L1 8-64, L2 32-128 iterations) on a stream, a stencil and
  a short-row kernel (rows of 7-9 elements, many arrays) at 2 and 4 threads per core;
- **C6** loop tails at lengths 1-33: x87 scalar vs aligned masked vs peel plus masked vs gather tail;
- **C7** a reduction with 1 vs 2 accumulators at 4 threads per core;
- **C9** unroll 1, 2 and 4 at 1 and 2 threads per core on a 4-cycle vector chain and a 12-cycle
  memory-operand chain;
- **C10** branch-mispredict penalty (random vs fixed direction, scalar loop).

existing format, accepted under ADR-0010 (median of group medians, spread reported). Keep the code
