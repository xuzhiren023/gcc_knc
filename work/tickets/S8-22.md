`-mphinix` is the owner's "best settings" flag: it must never be slower than plain `-O2 -march=knc`
beyond the A/A bias. mat16 at about x87 speed means its inner loop lost vectorization or became
something far worse. `-mphinix` is `-O3` plus defaults that are already on at `-O2` (S8-16), so the
cause is most likely an `-O3`-only transform on KNC (complete peeling/unrolling of the 16-wide loop,
SLP, alignment peeling or versioning with the dynamic cost model, predictive commoning, loop
distribution or interchange), or an interaction with the S8-15/S8-20 unroll.
