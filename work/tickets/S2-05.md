Backlog E2-03. S1-04 replaced `fcomi` with `fcompp`/`fnstsw`. Comparisons must still be correct for
NaN, signed zero and unordered cases, including `isless`, `isunordered` and friends, which must
not raise invalid on quiet NaNs. A `zmm` spill slot must be 64-byte aligned, because the KNC
aligned forms fault otherwise.

Acceptance (T1):

- [ ] Red first: T1 tests for each comparison form and for spill alignment, failing or shown
  missing before the change
- [ ] T1: every ordered and unordered comparison of `float`, `double` and `long double` uses the
  right compare form (`fucom` family for the quiet ones) and the right flags, checked against the
  donor's output; no `fcomi` anywhere
- [ ] T1: a function that spills a `zmm` value realigns its stack to 64 bytes, including when
  called with only 16-byte alignment
- [ ] Native, once the card is free: a table of NaN, -0.0, +0.0, infinities and ordinary values
  gives the IEEE results for every comparison
