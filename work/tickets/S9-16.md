Best-practice review, "Sources against our notes" points 1 and 4 (`docs/research/knc-best-practice-review.md`):

1. The ICC 16.0 guide says MIC data accesses must be naturally aligned (GUID-7E3E5C78-3F3B-4746-8C2D-A912EE1D6B38).
   GCC emits unaligned scalar moves (store merging, inline `memcpy`). Probe: 2-, 4- and 8-byte general
   register loads and stores, and x87 `fld`/`fst` of float and double, at every byte offset 0-63 and
   across a page boundary; report fault or not, and the time against aligned.
2. IMCI keeps denormals (our check and Intel agree; gaps G6 corrected). Probe the VPU time of packed add,
   mul and FMA with 0, 1 and 16 subnormal lanes against normal operands, at 1 and 4 threads per core.

Deliver two small C programs built with the GCC-KNC prefix, linted, with a runner; the lead runs them.
Write the result rows into `docs/research/knc-characterization.md` as C13 (unaligned scalar) and C14
(subnormal cost) after the lead's run (the lead adds the numbers).
