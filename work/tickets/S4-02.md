`docs/research/knc-masks.md` sections 1, 2 and 4. Rules: ADR-0006 forms for every shared change,
new patterns in `imci.md`, and `Bm` (never `m`) for packed-mode memory alternatives.

Deliver what ADR-0007 names for compares:
- `get_mask_mode` for V16SF, V8DF, V16SI and V8DI under KNC;
- compare expanders into `k` masks for every C comparison (`==`, `!=`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`,
  UNORDERED, ORDERED), signed and unsigned for V16SI. V8DI compares stay off, and so do the
  quiet UNLT/UNLE/UNGT/UNGE/LTGT/UNEQ until the card shows that masked-off NaN lanes raise
  nothing (ADR-0007 point 5). Exclude them in the comparison operand's predicate, so
  `expand_vec_cmp_expr_p` reports no support and those loops stay scalar (do not FAIL);
- mask logic, and moving masks between `k` registers, GPRs and memory, including spills and
  reloads under register pressure.

Blends and if-conversion are S4-03; this ticket only makes compare results exist and survive
register allocation.
