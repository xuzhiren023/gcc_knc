The lead's reading of S8-01's card results (`docs/research/knc-characterization.md`, "Sprint 8
tuning checks") found three checks that do not measure what the ticket asked:

1. **C4 (TLB reach):** every line sits at one page offset, so every point is an L2 hit and no TLB
   knee can show. Spread the touched lines over page offsets (and over L2 sets) so the working set
   is L2-resident but needs one TLB entry per 4 KB page; keep 128-448 KB per core at 1 and 4 threads.
2. **C5 short rows:** the 8 row arrays are 1 MiB apart and alias in one L1 set. Pad them (offset each
   array by a different multiple of 64 bytes plus a page stagger), keep the same distances.
3. **C9 memory-operand chain:** the folded load is off the recurrence. Put it on the chain (the
   loaded value feeds the next iteration's address or operand) so it is the 12-cycle chain asked for.
