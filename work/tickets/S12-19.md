whether GCC tried to vectorize it, why it refused, and whether a guard or a different cost would make it legal and
profitable.

prefix: collects `-fopt-info-vec-all` (missed and optimized, with reasons) and maps each report to its source loop;

sampled time; and classifies each hot loop as vectorized, missed-with-reason (alias, unknown stride, control flow,
cost model, unsupported operation, reduction order), or not a loop. Output a ranked table per program plus a corpus
summary: the reasons that cover the most sampled time. Run it on the 14 development programs only (never held-out,
ADR-0015). Host tests on a small fixture with known reasons. Write the findings in
`docs/research/s12-19-vec-triage.md`, ending with the 3 general compiler changes that would cover the most hot
missed time, each with its legality condition (guard or static proof) and how to measure it. No GCC change here.
