Backlog E5-03. A user (and the lead, when tuning) must see why each loop was or was not
vectorized for KNC, without reading RTL dumps.

Deliver, under `-fopt-info-vec` (and `-fopt-info-vec-missed` for rejections), KNC-specific notes for
each loop:
- the vector mode and factor, and whether a scalar peel, a masked epilogue or a scalar epilogue was
  chosen, and why (ADR-0007 point 7);
- gathers/scatters, converting accesses and reductions used;
- calls to helpers (libgcc or libm) in the loop body;
- the reason when KNC rejected a choice (for example the misaligned masked-access cost of
  ADR-0007 point 7, or an unsupported mode).
Add the notes in KNC-only code (`i386-knc.cc` cost hooks), not in shared vectorizer files.
