From S12-15 (item 02, "what we overlooked" row 2) and S12-10's G09. KNC programs often want different densities per
region (bandwidth-bound regions saturate at fewer workers per core, latency-bound ones need four). Our libgomp
picks one density per program. This is an OpenMP-panel improvement (ADR-0015: no primary M4 credit); draft for
Sprint 13 or M5. Scope when it comes up: measure density per region on the corpus OpenMP builds first (S12-12
style batch), then a bounded runtime policy with an explicit override and fallback, counting calibration cost.
