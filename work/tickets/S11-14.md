From the senior scope review (`docs/reviews/2026-11-12-s11-s13-scope-review.md`, findings 1, 2 and 8) and the
owner's decisions of 2026-11-12 (`docs/sprints/SPRINT-11.md` goal 1). Freeze how M4 is scored before Sprint 12
tunes anything; S11-11's harness then implements exactly this.

Deliver `docs/adr/0015-m4-scoring-and-measurement.md` (status: proposed; the lead accepts it) and a small,
host-tested scoring module (`tools/m4_score.py` or inside S11-11's harness by agreement on its ticket) covering:
- **The owner's decisions, verbatim in effect:** strict matched `-O2` vs `-O2` (GCC-KNC vs GCC 5.1.1) decides M4;
  `-O3`, `-mphinix` and PGO published alongside and labelled; 17 program units, one vote each; OpenMP primary at all
  hardware threads of the card or profile, one and two per core as side panels, serial programs one pinned worker;
  no exclusions after results; HPCG rule; held-out-triggered retry labelled an exposed-set confirmation; every run
  on the full 7120 and under `card_run --profile 3100` and `--profile 5100`, M4 must pass on each model, with the
  profiles' limits (cores and memory modelled, clock and per-core bandwidth not) stated; release with limits on a
  miss after the one retry.
- **Timed boundary per program** (launch-to-exit wall time on the card's monotonic clock as the score; initialization
  and algorithm phases reported when separable; staging, output transfer and validation outside), identical for
  both compilers; fresh-process latency vs resident throughput stated where it matters.
- **Launch protocol:** a pilot pass for buildability, correctness and duration, separate from the final gate; at the
  gate at least 5 independent paired launches per program, balanced AB/BA, identical inputs and resources within a
  pair, raw chronological times kept, one extension block fixed in advance for inconclusive results, never rerun
  only unfavourable pairs (ADR-0010 adapted to separate executables, as the review's finding 2 sets out).
- **Statistics:** speedup within each pair, per-program median of pair ratios, the median across the 17 programs;
  paired bootstrap over launches within programs for uncertainty; report T_new/T_old and its inverse; "more than 5%
  slower" means T_new/T_old > 1.05; the gate rule: one-sided 95% lower bound of the corpus median at least 1.25 on each
  model, plus the per-program regression review (intervals crossing a threshold flagged unresolved).
- **Correctness:** complete-output or reference/residual checks with predeclared tolerances and NaN/Inf rules; NPB
  verification, HPCG validity, exact compression round trips (cross-decoded with the other compiler's build where
  feasible), SQL expected results; a deliberately corrupted-result control the harness must reject.
- **Environment record:** clock/power policy, page mode, allocator and runtime settings, compiler and runtime revisions,
  flags, affinity, profile; invalid runs kept with reasons.
- **Held-out access separation:** the runner writes held-out timings, profiles and diagnostics only to a sealed
  lead-only output (`.work/_shared/m4-sealed/`); tuning tickets see readiness and pass/fail only.
