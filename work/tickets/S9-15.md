Best-practice review item 3 (`docs/research/knc-best-practice-review.md`): Intel's OpenMP runtime keeps
threads spinning about 200 ms between parallel regions (PRACE BPG §13.2.4-13.2.5); our KNC default is
3 ms of `delay` periods (S9-08, `knc/spincount.h`, scaled by the card's clock since S9-12). S9-08's card
data shows a spinning sibling with DELAY 128 costs the worker 1.1% (S9-11 makes 128 the default), so a
longer spin is cheap on the core; the cost is CPU time other processes cannot use. WRF runs about
9,850 parallel regions per step.

Deliver:
- A fork-join program: 240 threads, parallel regions separated by serial gaps of 1, 10 and 100 ms,
  timing the region entry; and a sibling-cost check as S9-08 measurement 1. Staged for the lead,
  with the spin time set by `GOMP_SPINCOUNT` at 3 ms, 30 ms and 200 ms equivalents, `GOMP_KNC_DELAY=128`.
- Do not change the default in this ticket: the lead sets it from the card result (ADR-0010 rules).
