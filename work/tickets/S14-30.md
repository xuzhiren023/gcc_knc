Lead 2026-11-16. PhiWeather card smokes (Miami 2024-01-06, steps 11-65): GCC-KNC WRF 4.8 builds (patched 16.2
890f58f8, S11-26 dm+sm 57be063f, and their -nc4 relinks) take 4.83 s/step against 3.995 s/step for PhiWeather's
"Sirocco" production image (1.21x). Owner rule: never slower than GCC 5.1.1 (`project-never-slower-than-donor`).
The comparison is not like for like: Sirocco carries hand IMCI kernels (about 4% of wall) and possibly other flags,
runtimes and threads per core. PhiWeather's candidate causes and the lead's status table are in
`docs/research/for-the-other-agent.md` (2026-11-16 entry).

Attribute first (model-first, tier standard; no compiler change in this ticket):
1. Same WRF source and namelist, two builds: GCC 5.1.1 with Sirocco's flags and without the hand kernels, and
   GCC-KNC at `-mphinix`, both at the same thread layout. Wait for PhiWeather's answer on Sirocco's compiler, flags,
   OpenMP/MPI runtime and threads per core (asked 2026-11-16).
2. Per-routine time shares on the card for both builds (gprof or sampled profile), staged as card jobs for the lead.
3. knc-twin 0.4.0 per hot routine (rk_update_scalar, advance_uv, advance_mu_t, mynn_tendencies, mp_thompson): price
   the heap automatic arrays (heap stubs, against `-fstack-arrays`), the short-tile epilogue (GitHub issue #32,
   partial-vector-usage 1 vs 2) and the scalar x87 tails (S12-27).
4. One table per cause: share of the gap, evidence, and the fix ticket it maps to (#32, S12-27, -fstack-arrays,
   -mphinix contents, hand kernels by design). Write it in `docs/research/s14-30-wrf-gap.md`.

Wait for PhiWeather's 25 h figure before step 2's card jobs. PhiWeather's folders are read-only. Submit with the
one-sentence note the owner reads.
