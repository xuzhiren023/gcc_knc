Lead 2026-11-16. PhiWeather card smokes (Miami 2024-01-06, steps 11-65): GCC-KNC WRF 4.8 builds (patched 16.2
890f58f8, S11-26 dm+sm 57be063f, and their -nc4 relinks) take 4.83 s/step against 3.995 s/step for PhiWeather's
"Sirocco" production image (1.21x). Owner rule: never slower than GCC 5.1.1 (`project-never-slower-than-donor`).
The comparison is not like for like: Sirocco carries hand IMCI kernels (about 4% of wall) and possibly other flags,
runtimes and threads per core. PhiWeather's candidate causes and the lead's status table are in
`docs/research/for-the-other-agent.md` (2026-11-16 entry).

   OpenMP/MPI runtime and threads per core (asked 2026-11-16).
