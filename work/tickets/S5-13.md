Backlog E4-02, part 1. Design: ADR-0008 point 6, as amended: every card condition holds.
Research: `docs/research/knc-gather-reduce.md` sections 1, 2 and 7 (ticket 4). Card facts:
`docs/research/card-runs-s5.md`.

Deliver, with no shared lines:
- `knc_gather<mode>` and `knc_scatter<mode>` insns for V16SF and V16SI. Each is one insn whose
  output is the instruction plus a `jknzd` retry loop, with:
  - an earlyclobber destination tied to the else value;
  - a V16SI index;
  - a mask that is not `k0`, and is consumed;
  - an unspec BLK memory reference;
  - `type "multi"`.
- `mask_gather_load`, `gather_load`, `mask_scatter_store` and `scatter_store` for V16SF and V16SI:
  - a V16SI offset;
  - scale 1, 2, 4 or 8 (address-width scaling was proven on the card);
  - signed offsets only.
- `ix86_knc_support_vector_misalignment` accepting gathers and scatters at element alignment.
