Owner, 2026-11-06: ADR-0010 amendment 2 is accepted (read it, and `docs/research/m2-gate-review.md`
sections 5-7, and `docs/research/m2-noise-diagnosis.md`). Tuning tickets (S6-05 first) need point 7:
before and after as two GCC-KNC builds of the same kernels in one process, on the same buffers,
timed in interleaved rounds. M2 needs the amendment-2 decision computed the same way every time.

The driver in `tests/acceptance/m2_kernels/` already interleaves x87, gcc and imci round by round
inside each group, with a 10 s warm-up, straggler fields and flags (commits bed5fa2 and the
amendment-2 commit). Build on it; do not change the kernels.

Deliver:
1. **Two GCC builds in one binary.** `build.py --before FOLDER` links a second copy of the GCC
   kernels from `FOLDER/kernels-gcc.o` (for example `.work/_shared/M2-final`), with its symbols
   renamed by the cross `k1om-mpss-linux-objcopy --redefine-sym` (`m2_<k>_gcc` to
   `m2_<k>_gccbefore`), as a fourth build `gcc_before`. Without `--before` the binary is as today.
   Correctness checks every build, including `gcc_before`.
2. **Per-round order drawn per round** (ADR-0010 amendment 2 point 5): a seeded shuffle per round
   (seed printed, derived from the launch number), replacing the fixed rotation, so every build is
   timed first in some rounds of every launch.
3. **Per-round ratios.** For every timed round print `round` lines with `gcc_over_gcc_before` (when
   linked) and the gain fraction from that round's batches; per launch print a `compare` line with
   the median ratio and a bootstrap percentile 95% interval over that launch's rounds.
4. **`decide.py`** (host Python, no card): reads a set of output files and prints (a) the M2 decision
   per amendment 2 clauses (a), (b) and (c) with the launch values, range, minimum, lower bound,
   bootstrap interval of the median gain fraction and regime-step count, the sequential step when
   it applies, and invalid launches named with the reason; (b) for a tuning comparison, point 7's
   figure: the median of per-round `T_after/T_before` over all launches, its bootstrap interval,
   per-launch signs, and accept/reject against a `--min-effect` argument.
5. README: the new options, lines and how the lead runs a tuning comparison and an M2 block.
