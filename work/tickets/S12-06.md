Sprint 12 improvement theme 1 of 3 (docs/M4-DEFINITION.md, S12 row; docs/research/m4-baseline.md "At most three
general mechanisms", priority 1). Qualified exposure: NPB-EP scalar transform/call site 51.2 s, EP log path 36.8 s,
IS randlc 5.2 s; GCC-KNC is only 1.10x over GCC 5.1.1 on EP and IS, against 1.5-3.9x elsewhere.

Use lane 1's installed prefix `.work/_shared/gcc-knc-16.2.0` (knc 619e14b479a) for phase 1; the lead builds this
lane's tree (slot 2) on request, per AGENTS.md (commit, then ask on the ticket).

Phase 1, proof (stop here if the premise fails). On the EP and IS hot regions named in the baseline, compare GCC-KNC
and GCC 5.1.1 code at -O2 and separate four costs: x87/VPU transfers through memory, int/FP conversions, call
save/restore around math calls, and the math bodies themselves (libm bodies are out of scope). Use small host-built
reproducers of the regions, not benchmark-name matching. Stage one paired diagnostic batch (at most 30 min of card
time) for the lead: reproducers plus a no-call scalar control and an already-vectorized control. Write the result in
docs/research/s12-06-scalar-fp.md: which cost is compiler-removable and its share of the exposure.

Phase 2, only if phase 1 finds a compiler-removable cost of at least 3 s of EP+IS exposure: a general backend or
middle-end change (cost model, conversion or transfer pattern, call-boundary register choice) selected by code
property, never by benchmark name. Strict FP: same precision, rounding, exceptions and evaluation order; no fast-math,
FTZ or reassociation. ABI unchanged.

Acceptance:
- Red-then-green T1 test(s) on the intended generated code (the transfer/conversion/save sequence that goes away).
- T0-T2 and the x86 identity check (ADR-0006) pass; no new ICE in the ICE sweep inputs.
- Stage for the lead a paired before/after GCC-KNC A/B on NPB-EP and NPB-IS plus two controls (PB-gemm and zlib),
  serial -O2, full/3100/5100 profiles, through the M4 harness (tools/corpus), with checks on. The note reports the
  paired medians; a gain below 2% on EP is reported as "no benefit", not tuned further.
- No held-out program is built, read or run (ADR-0015).
