Backlog E3-06. Milestone M1 asks whether the i386 subtarget is still cheaper than a separate
backend. Measure and compare. The owner delegated the M1 call to the lead on 2026-11-03 ("Decide M1
and s3-11"). The lead decides from this note against the criteria below, which were fixed before
the evidence came in. The owner's direction of 2026-11-03 (ADR-0006: share i386, but correct and
portable) replaces `docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md` section 6's 300-line cap: lines are reported, and ADR-0006
form is required.

**Go** (accept M1) only if every item holds:

1. `m1_affine`: every README row is green with GCC 16, the card rows from S3-10 included.
2. S3-10's card batch: every program passes. That covers the Sprint 3 vector checks, unaligned
   pairs, TLS (S3-14), complex values and the rounding mode (S3-15), and the inexact-input affine
   variant (within 1 ULP everywhere).
3. No ICE class from SSE or AVX assumptions in shared i386 code is open at `-O2 -ftree-vectorize`
   and `-O3`. Other ICE classes are listed, each with a ticket.
4. x86_64: identical assembly at the final head against stock 16.2, at `-O2` and `-O2 -mavx512f`.
   Any difference our shared lines cause is a defect and must be fixed first. Differences with
   another cause (paths, build IDs) are named as such.
5. Portability: every drill conflict onto `upstream/master` is textual (context next to our hunk),
   with none in our logic. Trunk with our diff builds C-only, and T1 and T2 give the same results
   there as on 16.2. If not, each difference has a cause and a ticket, and none is a design clash.
6. Shared changes follow ADR-0006 rules 1-4 after S3-13: hooks only, bodies and prototypes in
   `i386-knc.*`. Report the lines and hunks.
7. The separate backend's estimated cost, with its method stated, exceeds the subtarget's measured
   cost so far plus its expected cost to reach M2.

**Adjust:** M1 waits. Use this when an item fails but the fix fits the i386 path: the failures
become tickets ahead of Sprint 4's work, and the lead decides again when they merge.

**Stop:** M1 fails on a structural reason. Examples: x86_64 differences that hooks cannot remove,
trunk breaks that keep recurring in our logic, or item 7 reversed. The lead writes the case and
sends it to the owner, because changing backends changes direction.

On go, the lead updates `work/milestones.json`, approves with a plain note, and makes a local
milestone commit and tag in both repositories. Nothing is pushed.

Deliverable: `docs/research/m1-result.md`, short. Separate measured from estimated.
