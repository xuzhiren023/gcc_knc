1. **Auto-parallelization** (`-ftree-parallelize-loops=N`, GCC splits countable, independent loops
   across threads through libgomp, no pragmas in the source). Take three loop shapes with no OpenMP:
   a streaming triad, a stencil, and a reduction; build at `-O2 -march=knc` with N = 1 (off), 60,
   120 and 240, plus the same loops written with `#pragma omp parallel for` as the reference. Report
   which loops GCC parallelized (`-fopt-info-loop-optimized`) and stage a timing runner (ADR-0010
   pairing) at those thread counts. If a loop is not parallelized, say why from the dump.
2. **AutoFDO feasibility** (`-fauto-profile`, backlog E7-08 "parked until perf sampling works"):
   - A card probe script that reports, without changing anything on the card: whether a `perf` binary
     exists, the kernel's `perf_event` support (`/proc/sys/kernel/perf_event_paranoid`,
     `/sys/bus/event_source/devices/`), and whether a `perf_event_open` cycles sampling event can be
     opened by our user (a 20-line C test).
   - From the ISA manual and the sources in `docs/research/knc-intrinsics-sources.md`: whether KNC
     has any branch-record facility (like x86 LBR) that AutoFDO needs for good profiles. Facts and
     page references only (ADR-0013).
   - A verdict: feasible now, feasible with IP-only samples (say how weak that is), or blocked.
