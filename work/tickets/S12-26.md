PhiWeather, 2026-11-14 (reply to the lead's latency-hiding note). Their hottest routines by critical-path share (Houston
25 h, 8x15 at 2 threads/core): mp_thompson 5.81% (scalar exp/log/pow, divides, sqrt, per-column dependent chains),
advect_scalar_pd 2.54%, mynnedmf 2.27% (tridiagonal recurrences, divides), rk_update_scalar 2.00%, advance_uv 1.76%;
RRTMG lw/sw significant on radiation steps. Groups: PBL 11.0%, microphysics 8.7%, solve_em tile loops 29.4%, halo and
MPI wait 18.2%. Their measurement: short-row WRF loops are latency-bound (about 8 GB/s without prefetch vs 87+ GB/s
streaming; vprefetch0 gave 2.4x). Sources on their side (read-only for us): PhiWeather
docs/implementation/HOTLOOP_PARALLEL_STUDY_20261112.md and KNC_DECK_OPTIMIZATION_CYCLE_20261112.md.

After S11-26 stages the clean -mphinix build and S12-19's tool is merged: run the triage on that build for, in order,
Thompson, MYNN-EDMF, advect_scalar_pd and rk_update_scalar, then RRTMG lw/sw. For each hot loop: vectorized or the
missed reason, and dependent chain vs independent iterations; whether GCC-KNC already emitted software prefetch for
it (-O2+ default, near/far 16/32 lines) and, if not, why. Write `docs/research/s12-26-wrf-scalar.md` and send the
classified list to the lead.

Arms (only after PhiWeather picks them with the lead): per-file builds with transform 1 (unroll independent
iterations by 2) and 2 (hoist conversions and index math; exact invariant hoists), plus the S12-25
threads-per-core=2 arm. Transforms 3 (x*(1/y)) and 4 (split partial sums) are NOT approved until PhiWeather's owner
says so. No fast-math, FP environment preserved. Host builds only; message the lead before each build.
