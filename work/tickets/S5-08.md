`docs/research/knc-conversions.md` sections 1, 2 and 6 (ticket S5-gcc-A). Read them first,
including 2.4 on the four named insns.

Deliver:
- In `imci.md`, before any use: `*knc_floatv16siv16sf2` and `*knc_floatunsv16siv16sf2`
  (`vcvtfxpnt{,u}dq2ps $0`), and `*knc_fix{,uns}_truncv16sfv16si2` (`vcvtfxpntps2{,u}dq $3`).
  Memory operands are aligned and full-width only (`Bm`).
- In `sse.md`: the named-insn exception, used exactly as ADR-0008 point 5 allows. Add one
  mode-tested KNC condition term each to `floatv16siv16sf2`, `fix_truncv16sfv16si2` and
  `fixuns_truncv16sfv16si2`. Do not touch `vec_unpacks_lo_v16sf`; that waits for part B.
- Enable `floatuns` through an expander-only iterator copy plus a condition term.
- Rules (a)-(d) of point 5 must hold. Show (a) and (b) in the submit note, with the `imci.md`
  line numbers ahead of `sse.md`.
- Enable float-to-int and int-to-float unconditionally. The convert probe showed that
  `vcvtfxpntps2dq $3` raises IE for NaN, infinity and finite out-of-range inputs and PE for
  inexact ones, so it is IEEE-correct under `-ftrapping-math` (`docs/research/card-runs-s5.md`).
  Use immediates 0-3 only; 4-7 raise SIGILL.
