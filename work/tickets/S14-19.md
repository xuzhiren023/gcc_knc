Found 2026-11-16 by the knc-boinc mini-project (C:/Users/xuzhi/Documents/knc-boinc, ticket S1-04, Codex engineer,
lead reviewed). Running crlibm `log_rn` from a GCC-KNC card binary, the twin returned 0x43c628b76e3a7b61 for
log(1.0) (expected +0.0). First divergence: `fildl 0x8(%rsp)` (bytes `db 44 24 08`, DB /0 = signed m32int load).
The twin's isa.py gives fildl mem_size=8, so it reads stale high bytes; source inspection also finds filds (DF /0,
m16int) given 4 bytes. A local diagnostic that changed only fildl's width to 4 made the full log(1.0) match x86 exactly.

Evidence in knc-boinc: `twin/fildl-repro.json` (4-instruction standalone repro, result 0x43cff80000000000 vs 0),
`probes/fildl_host.c` (x86 executes the same fildl: 0), `twin/fildl-width-diagnostic.json`, `twin/log-diagnosis.json`.

Fix the widths of fildl/filds (and audit the other x87 integer forms: fistl/fistpl/fists/fistps/fisttp*, fiadd*/fimul*
etc.) in the knc-twin ISA table, add the repro as a functional test, rerun `scripts/validate_heldout.py`, and bump the
engine version. Any held-out or calibration result that executed these forms functionally must be rechecked.
