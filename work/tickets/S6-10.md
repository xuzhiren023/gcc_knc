Two failure shapes in the same class:
- `pr109011-4.c`, `pr109011-5.c` (`for ... p[i] = __builtin_ffsll (q[i])`): "unrecognizable insn"
  `(set (reg:DI) (if_then_else:DI (eq (reg:CCZ flags) ...)))` in vregs. KNC has no CMOV
  (S1, `TARGET_CMOVE` is false), so whatever emits a conditional move for `ffs` must take the
  non-CMOV path under KNC.
- `vect-63.c`, `vect-outer-2b.c`, `vect-outer-2d.c`, `vect-double-reduc-3.c`,
  `vect-double-reduc-7.c`, and `vect-simd-17.c` (moved from S6-09: after S6-09's `vec_series` fix it reaches
  the same crash): "insn does not satisfy its constraints" in cprop_hardreg, a
  `knc_movhi` of `-1` into `k1`. A k register cannot take an immediate; the constant must go
  through a GPR (`kmov` from a register), so a pattern or a constraint lets cprop propagate the
  constant where it cannot go.
