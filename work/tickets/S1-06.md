Backlog E1-05. Port the donor's `gcc/config/i386/linuxk1om.h` defaults into the
`linux-k1om.h` from S1-02 (S0-05 inventory: port). The donor's values:

- Dynamic loader `/lib64/ld-linux-k1om.so.2`
- `--march=k1om` passed to the assembler, `-m elf_k1om` to the linker
- `long double` is x87 80-bit in 16 bytes (`__LDBL_MANT_DIG__` 64, `__SIZEOF_LONG_DOUBLE__` 16),
  and `__SIZEOF_FLOAT128__` is 16. The backlog's "128-bit long double" wording was wrong

GCC 16 has no `libgcc` for this target until E2-05. For this ticket only, link through the
donor's `crtbegin.o`, `crtend.o` and `libgcc` by `-B` to the donor's
`lib/gcc/k1om-mpss-linux/5.1.1/`, and say so in the test. Do not copy them into the fork.

Acceptance (T1 and T2, with `tools/check --compiler gcc16`):

- [ ] Red first: a T1 test of the macros above, and a T2 link of an integer-only `main` that
  fails before the change
- [ ] With no extra flags beyond that `-B`, the linked program has ELF machine 181 (K1OM), the
  interpreter above, and every libc symbol version within glibc 2.21, against the card sysroot
- [ ] `-v` shows `--march=k1om` to `as` and `-m elf_k1om` to `ld`
- [ ] No native run here: the card is on the owner's hold, and `hello` on the card is E2-06
