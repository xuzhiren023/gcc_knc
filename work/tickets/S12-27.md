Owner, 2026-11-14: programs should not need hand-applied flags or source rewrites to hide KNC latency; GCC-KNC must
take the latency character into account when it compiles. Facts (docs/research/knc-references.md, S12-14 audit
B01/B04/B05/B15, knc-costs.md): one hardware thread issues at most every other cycle; dependency latencies are 4
(vector add/mul/FMA, scalar add recurrence), 6 (permute/swizzle/reciprocal), 7 (double-to-int), about 10 (scalar
imul, being measured in S12-21) and about 38 (x87 divide/sqrt, unpipelined); cross-domain moves go through memory.
PhiWeather's WRF profile (S12-26) shows hot scalar, latency-bound loops (Thompson, MYNN, advection).

Draft until S12-21 phase 1 reports. Scope then, general and keyed on code properties only (never a program or
file name): (1) in -O2/-O3 -march=knc, unroll small scalar (non-vectorized) loops whose iterations are independent
by the factor that covers the dominant dependency latency at the assumed threads per core (from S12-21's
scheduling model), within a code-size cap, so the scheduler can interleave them; (2) make sure loop-invariant
int-to-float conversions and index multiplies are hoisted (check where they are not, and why); (3) keep FP
semantics strict: no reassociation, no reciprocal division, no split sums. Test inputs: the 14 development
programs, the S12-02 generality set, and reduced WRF loops from S12-26 as unscored checks (never the held-out
programs). Red-then-green T1 tests on the intended unroll/hoist decisions; T0-T2, x86 identity (ADR-0006), ICE
sweeps; stage paired A/B (before/after) on affected programs plus two controls at full/3100/5100.
