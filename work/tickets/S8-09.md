Backlog E5-18. Generic GCC `-O2` leaves many KNC loops scalar where ICC `-O2` vectorizes. For
`-march=knc`, choose the `-O2` defaults for the vectorizer cost model (`very-cheap` vs `cheap` vs
`dynamic`), loop versioning for stride and alias, and predictive commoning, from T1 scans over the
M2 kernels and the corpus loops (S0-07), and the lead's paired card runs.

Decided by the lead (2026-11-08): `-ftrapping-math` stays the default. The VPU cannot trap, but
the flags are observable through `fetestexcept` (ADR-0003), so `-fno-trapping-math` is not a safe
default; do not change it here.
