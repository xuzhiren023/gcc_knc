Backlog E1-02, under ADR-0002: KNC is its own ISA. Add `TARGET_ISA_KNC` with option handling
in `i386-options.cc`, flags in `i386.opt` and macros in `i386-c.cc` (S0-05 inventory, rows for
`i386.opt`, `i386.h` and `i386-c.c`). It never sets `TARGET_AVX512F` or any SSE, AVX or MMX flag.
The `k1om` target defaults to it.

The donor's behaviour, measured by the lead on 2026-11-02, is the reference except where
ADR-0002 says otherwise:

- Defaults `-march=knc -mtune=knc -mfpmath=387`; no SSE, MMX, CX16, FXSR or SAHF flag enabled
- Predefines `__KNC__`, `__k1om__`, `__k1om` and `__x86_64__`; no `__SSE__`, `__SSE2__` or `__MMX__`
- `int f(int a,int b,int c){return a<b?c:a;}` at `-O2` branches (`jl`), with no `cmov`
- `long double` `a<b` at `-O2` uses `fucompp`, `fnstsw`, `testb`, `sete`; no `fcomi`, no `sahf`
- `__atomic_thread_fence(__ATOMIC_SEQ_CST)` emits `lock; orl $0, (%rsp)`, not `mfence`
- Differs on purpose: the donor accepts `-msse2` silently; ADR-0002 requires an error

  `__AVX512F__` is
- [ ] `-msse`, `-msse2`, `-mavx`, `-mavx512f` and `-mmmx` with `-march=knc` are errors
- [ ] No `cmov`, `fcomi`, `fucomi` or `mfence` in the three code tests, with the donor's forms present
- [ ] A seeded bug once, such as leaving CMOV enabled, is caught by a test; say so in the evidence
